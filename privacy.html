Load Forge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Summary

Load Forge has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect personal data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your saved designs and your Bass Match settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent to our server

To compute a design or run Bass Match, the app sends the driver name (or Thiele-Small values you typed), the box values and the search settings you chose to the Load Forge server (hosted on Google Cloud, Belgium, EU). The server answers and discards the request: it does not store your designs or your settings. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical request logs (for example the IP address and time of a request) for a limited period, for security and operation only; we do not use them to identify you and do not link them to any profile. The app sends no name, email, location, advertising identifier or analytics.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties and use no third-party analytics or advertising. Hosting is provided by Google Cloud, which processes requests on our behalf.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete. For any question write to info@load-forge.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
