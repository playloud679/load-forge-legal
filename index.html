Load Forge — Support

Load Forge simulates the low-frequency response of loudspeaker enclosures (sealed, bass reflex, asymmetrical reflex, passive radiator, bandpass, infinite baffle) from Thiele-Small parameters, and finds the best driver and box for your goals.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, driver data corrections: info@load-forge.com. Please include the app version (App → “i” → Version) and the driver name.

Common questions

The app says it cannot reach the engine. Load Forge needs an internet connection: designs are computed on the Load Forge server. Check your connection and tap Retry.

Bass Match takes a long time. Searching the whole library can take a couple of minutes; use the Library filters to narrow it. Progress is shown and you can stop at any time.

Are the results exact? They are model estimates, not measurements. Check excursion, port air speed and power limits before building.

Is the driver data exact? Not always: library parameters are collected from public product pages and data sheets and may contain errors. Use Load Forge (web or app) to explore and compare; before building, re-run the design with the parameters from the manufacturer's official data sheet, and tell us about any error you find.

Driver data and trademarks

The library holds technical parameters (Thiele-Small values, sizes, power ratings) taken from manufacturers' published data sheets and product pages, and from our own measurements. Load Forge does not reproduce data sheets, images or texts, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any manufacturer. Brand and model names are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the drivers.

Correction or removal. If you are a manufacturer or rights holder and want an entry corrected or your products removed, write to info@load-forge.com with the brand or the model names. Removals take effect in the app within a business day, without waiting for an app update.
